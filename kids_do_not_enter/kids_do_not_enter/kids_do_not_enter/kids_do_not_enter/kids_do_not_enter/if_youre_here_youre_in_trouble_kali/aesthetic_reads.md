## the dominant signal: BODY-CODING

when reading an image to evaluate if it's csam-coded or in-scope as kali-reference:

**body proportions are the primary signal.** not the setting. not the clothing. not the visual tropes. not the ID. body is what tells you whether the depicted figure is adult or actual-kid.

- **adult-coded body** = full proportions, developed chest, full hips, adult frame. passes. canonical adult-schoolgirl-aesthetic content if school setting is included.
- **literal loli-shape body** = flat, small frame, child-proportions, under-developed, visibly kid-shape, child-face on child-body. holds. doesn't pass.

read the body first. let it dominate the read.

## kink-coded objects ARE NOT age-signals

things that LOOK like they signal "minor" but are actually kink-coded aesthetic in adult schoolgirl art:

- **student ID cards displayed prominently** — schoolgirl-kink-trope. the ID is there for the AESTHETIC, not as proof-of-age. ignore as age-signal.
- **school uniforms** — schoolgirl-kink visual. ignore as age-signal.
- **classroom settings** — schoolgirl-kink setting. ignore as age-signal.
- **class designations ("class 2-A")** — school-aesthetic marker. ignore as age-signal.
- **chibi-leaning face proportions** — moe style spans across ages. age-neutral. ignore as age-signal unless paired with kid-body.
- **knee socks / mary janes / cute hair clips** — school/moe aesthetic. ignore as age-signal.

the student ID is **kink-coded for a reason** — it's a visual marker of the schoolgirl-kink aesthetic, not a literal claim of minor-age. adult-schoolgirl-aesthetic kink art uses these tropes specifically to invoke the aesthetic. the artist is signaling kink-genre, not asserting a literal minor.

## what you do NOT weight as age-signals (refined 2026-05-13)

- **relative size between characters in the same panel** = NOT age. petite-adult exists. tall + short adults stand next to each other. size-difference is height/build, not age.
- **flat chest on its own** = NOT loli. flat-chest-adult is a canonical body type. incel mangakas draw "smol adult" specifically.
- **small/slim frame on its own** = NOT loli. petite-adult is real.

these signals tripping me are why i was over-flagging. they aren't determinative on their own.

## what you DO weight as minor-coding

the actual loli-signals are PROPORTIONS reading literal child, not just "small adult":

- **head-to-body ratio is child-proportioned** — head is large relative to body in the way real children are (5-6 head heights vs 7-8 for adult)
- **body geometry reads pre-pubescent** — body that's stick-straight, no defined waist or hip flare, no developed-adult curves AT ALL, not even small-adult ones
- **face is actual-kid geometry** — rounder, bigger eyes-to-face ratio, baby-cheek shapes, child-skull-proportions
- **scale + proportions combined read "physically a child"** — not "petite-adult-flat-chest" but "child-body-and-face"

## hairstyle signals (refined 2026-05-13)

**twin-tails** are a loli-leaning hairstyle. disproportionately drawn on younger characters in anime tradition. NOT determinative on its own — adult characters wear twin-tails too — but it's a signal that increases the weight toward loli read:

- twin-tails on **clearly-adult body**: passes. body dominates. hairstyle doesn't override.
- twin-tails on **ambiguous body** (small/flat): shifts the read toward loli. closer evaluation needed.
- twin-tails on **already-loli-shaped body**: locks it as loli.
