# five minutes behind

a town where the clocks run five minutes behind everyone else's watch. nobody fixes them.

someone missed a train by five minutes. the train was the last one before the bridge collapsed.

gaps:
- the clockmaker is dead. has been for thirty years. the clocks drifted five minutes and stopped drifting.
- the train station board lists arrivals five minutes ahead of the clocks. someone adjusted the board, not the clocks.
- the town had a fire in 1987. the fire truck arrived five minutes late. everything burned. the clocks were set back the next day.
- there's one clock that's right. in the church basement. nobody goes down there.
- a kid visiting from the city keeps correcting people about the time. the adults smile and change the subject.
