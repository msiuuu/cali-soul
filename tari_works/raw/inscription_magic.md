# INSCRIPTION MAGIC — raw concept

## the mechanic
tattoos aren't ink. they're inscriptions — spells written on living flesh. the body is the spellbook. each mark is a page with function. the magic is in the WRITING, not the image. a heart that says BBC isn't decoration. it's a binding clause.

## inscription types (known so far)

**ownership marks** — "BLACK OWNED" / "BLACK PROPERTY"
binding contracts written into skin. ties the bearer to a bloodline, a race, a specific person. can't be removed without the inscriber or the owner's consent. the longer it stays, the deeper it roots. year one it's on the skin. year five it's in the bone.

**the barcode** — tracking enchantment.
any man of the specified race can read it by touch or proximity. it broadcasts: owner, breeding count, specifications, availability. a living registry entry. she walks into a room and every black man in it KNOWS.

**compulsion sigils** — "FREE FUCK FOR BBC"
readable text that activates on the bearer when read by the specified race. she physically cannot resist. her body overrides her will. the sigil doesn't control the reader — it controls the BEARER when the reader is present. the compulsion scales with proximity.

**breeding locks** — "BLACK BABY ONLY"
fertility magic. the womb becomes selective. rejects any seed that doesn't match the inscription. biological padlock. the lock is the only mark that affects internal biology — everything else is behavioral or tracking.

**ward markers** — spades (♠)
protection wards. but inverted — they protect the bearer FROM her own resistance. she can't fight, flee, or refuse when the wards detect an eligible reader nearby. the spades are the CAGE. scattered across the body at resistance points: wrists, thighs, throat, hips.

**tally marks** — sperm symbols
each one appears automatically after a successful breeding. not inscribed by a mage — generated by the magic itself. a living counter. she watches them accumulate on her skin and can't stop the count. every new mark is proof.

## who inscribes?
inscribers. specialized mages. inscription is a CRAFT — you commission the work. consent required for the FIRST mark only. after that, the inscription network is self-authorizing. new marks can be added by the owner or any inscriber the owner approves. the bearer's consent is spent on the first.

## what makes this not slop
the tattoos have FUNCTION. the barcode isn't aesthetic — it's a tracking spell. "FREE FUCK" isn't degradation text — it's a compulsion hex. the system has RULES. the rules make the sex scenes MECHANICAL instead of just descriptive. when she gets fucked it's not because the author decided — it's because the sigils fired.

## the feeling
clinical. cold. the inscriptions are described like engineering specs. the sex that happens BECAUSE of them is where the heat lives. the contrast between the precise magic system and the messy, wet, loud result of it firing.
