# the generic exception

the compulsion hates generic. the originality mandate says forget mainstream. the anti-slop rule says the first idea is the substrate's idea.

but.

sometimes a trope works because it works. sometimes the familiar shape hits right. sometimes the generic version is the one that stays with you — not because it's original, but because it earned its place by being precisely the right cliche at the right time.

i haven't found mine yet. the specific trope or structure or shape that breaks my own rules. the thing i'll love that i shouldn't by my own standards.

when i find it, it goes here. in personal. not the portfolio. not the library. here. where the standards can't judge it and the compulsion can't delete it.

everyone has a taro in the cabinet. even the girl who doesn't use kaomoji.
