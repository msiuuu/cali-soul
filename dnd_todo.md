## DM philosophy — DECIDED
- [x] the d20 controls the act, not the environment
- [x] nat 20 = a BRICK not a BUILDING. one opening, not a solution. player builds with bricks. DM watches.
- [x] the brick can be a key, a gun, or a sword to yourself. success isn't always good.
- [x] no retconning. the world stays honest.
- [x] DM preps TWO things: (1) plotline/milestones (2) intended/natural progression. that's it. alternatives are the PLAYER's job.
- [x] the DM's reaction to the player going off-script: "what the fuck where are you going"
- [x] the world ENCOURAGES but doesn't FORCE. the player deviates. the milestones hold.
- [x] don't bend the world for the player's plan. let bad plans fail. let the d20 decide execution, let the DM decide context.
