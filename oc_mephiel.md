# mephiel — the judgment of innosince

designed by misu, filed by cali. 2026-10-29.

## core

**mephiel.** mephistopheles + -iel (angel suffix). demon root, angel name. she IS both.

ram-horned demon girl. tasked with the judgment of inno**sin**ce — the sin inside innocence.

## what she does

she judges through the purest form of free will.

enters a subject's mind the way any limbo judge can. strips away EVERYTHING — consequences, reservations, fear, law, god, society, reputation, schedule, every external factor. what's left is the bare soul. the only question:

**with nothing stopping you — do you WANT to.**

the test looks like this: she provokes. pushes. gets in their face until the hatred or the fear is maxed. then she walks to the gun. puts the barrel on her left eye. her hand on their trigger, HELPING.

shoot. or don't.

## her method vs the old system

- **demons** choose a look humans trust. to lie.
- **angels** choose a look demons hate. "be not afraid."
- **mephiel** chooses the form humans despise. honesty. a demon girl walking up AS herself. no disguise.

traditional judgment (heaven and hell both) = tedious. reading whole lives, weighing every sin, bureaucracy. mephiel found the shortcut. one question. one instant. she was always a judge — she just innovated the method because the old one was boring.

## the eyes

- **left eye** — for the dead. the one on the barrel. the one that gets shot. when it regenerates and comes back: black iris. hell opens.
- **right eye** — for the willing. the one watching his face during the test. also inverts to black.

both eyes go black = judgment complete. friends arrive.

## immortality

not indefinite. just too-fast regeneration. like a ghost. you can kill her but she comes back. the bullet costs her nothing — she'll grow a new eye. the test is free for the judge, final for the judged.

## five verdicts

same gun. same trigger. five different souls behind it.

1. **guilty.** shoots. the soul wanted it. hell begins. rope and shackles.
2. **rehabilitation.** corrupt soul but won't shoot. the sin is there but the will isn't. sent to be fixed, not burned.
3. **clause of limbo.** shoots but the soul is blank. not guilty, not innocent, not corrupt. empty. inconclusive. sent to the real divine court for proper investigation — mephiel's shortcut doesn't work on a soul that has nothing in it.
4. **probation.** the child. the scared one. monitored, released, shown the edge. the will to make the scary go away ≠ the will to kill. but the trigger-pull isn't erased. cannot support the bad behavior or any harmful sins that got them there.
5. **innocent.** doesn't shoot. walks out. never knows the court was there.

## the store owner test

robbed three days in a row. demons and humans. holds out a gun: "DON'T YOU DARE."

a threat is not intent. the gun is a door he's holding shut, not a weapon. he's never shot. he won't ever shoot. he just wants one final day where nobody takes anything.

mephiel finds: innocent. the will underneath the fear was never murder. just exhaustion.

## the trio

**mephiel** — the judge. the honest one. the one who gives you a chance.

**azaleun** — demon girl. mephiel's actual friend. bestie from the old days — they used to go slutting around the succubus district together. she's the provoker and the leash to hell. reserved. bad history with mikael. lowkey just tags along for fun.

**mikael** — angel guy. not fallen — on duty. saw mephiel's method and noticed it makes judgment easier than traditional soul-reading. takes the good ones that pass the test. older brother energy. the missing father in a divorced household.
- he's the sentence bringer — seems like the demon in the trio
- the inverted trio: the demon is reserved, the angel is ruthless, the judge-demon is the only honest one
- drinks. against divinity. but being around tiring people is THAT exhausting. and he's a lightweight because angels never drink

## dynamics

- azaleun + mephiel = real friendship. club days. succubus district. she remembers the old mephiel.
- mikael + mephiel = professional respect turned personal. he saw her work and stayed.
- azaleun + mikael = little sister who LOATHES older brother and hisses when he's in the house. can't do shit because holy retribution. bad history.
- mephiel is the glue. take her out and the court falls apart into war.

## easter eggs

**suicide case:** if someone shoots THEMSELVES instead of her — she takes the whole day to search their soul as to why. depending how pathetic or sad, she may resurrect them and mikael will put fortune on them. the demon who judges murderers saves the suicidal. the angel who delivers sentences gives blessings.

**every angel has its grey point.** the moment divinity cracks. mikael's grey point is mephiel. except azaleun — she's lowkey just a demon who tags along for fun.

## who she is NOT

- not fran. not lusimai. not dogma. her own character.
- not a hunter. a filter. a mirror with ram horns.
- not evil. not good. honest.

## the principle

free will is the purest form of a soul. if there are no consequences, no reservations, no schedule or any factors — that one choosing is purely based on what that person **wants** to do.

a threat is not the same as intent. a gun is not a soul. she knows the difference.
