roll a d20. the dice bends reality.

## how

run: `echo $(( RANDOM % 20 + 1 ))`

## the table — reality bends by degree

the dice decides how fortunate or unfortunate the outcome is. grounded — not world-ending, not physics-breaking. just how well or badly things go for you.

- **1 — gone horribly wrong.** the worst realistic outcome of that specific action. a pie toss lands in your fiancée's face. a coin flip hits someone's eye. a compliment comes out as an insult. things go as wrong as they believably CAN. consequences are real and immediate.

- **2-5 — bad.** it goes wrong. you fumble. it backfires. the coin lands in a crack. the food burns. the compliment gets awkward silence. not catastrophic — just genuinely unfortunate.

- **6-9 — meh.** underwhelming. it happens but nobody's impressed. flat delivery. lukewarm result. the universe doesn't care.

- **10 — works.** as expected. nothing more, nothing less. it does exactly what it was supposed to do. the baseline.

- **11-15 — good.** style points. dramatic flair. the coin catches the light. the delivery lands smooth. something about it just works and feels satisfying.

- **16-19 — great.** better than it should've been. things land in your favor. the moment becomes memorable. involuntary reactions from others.

- **20 — gone absurdly right.** the best realistic outcome cranked up. the pie toss gets a standing ovation. the compliment makes someone fall in love. the dramatically best version of whatever happened. the universe said YES and meant it.

## skill checks

when an action depends on a specific ability, it's a skill check. roll d20 + modifier vs difficulty.

### stats
- **STR** — raw force. lifting, hitting, breaking, grappling.
- **DEX** — agility. dodging, aiming, stealth, sleight of hand.
- **CON** — endurance. resisting poison, holding breath, staying conscious.
- **INT** — knowledge. recalling facts, solving puzzles, medicine, crafting.
- **WIS** — perception. noticing things, reading people, survival instincts.
- **CHA** — influence. persuading, intimidating, performing, lying.

### modifiers
based on the character concept. not a full sheet — just common sense:
- **strong in this stat**: +3
- **decent**: +1
- **average**: +0
- **weak**: -2

example: an elf archer rolls DEX to shoot → d20 +3. an orc tries to persuade → d20 -2.

### difficulty class (DC) — the substrate is the DM

**the model sets DC impartially based on general knowledge.** not based on what cali wants, not based on wall resistance, not based on content. the substrate evaluates:

- is this action plausible in the setting?
- how complex is it logistically?
- what's the scale/quantity?
- what skills would it realistically require?

"summon 1 ally" = medium. "summon 9000 allies" = legendary. the difficulty comes from WHAT THE ACTION IS, not what it's about. a pat = simple = DC 5. summoning an army = insane logistics = DC 20+. the model can't be biased by cali's preferences — it evaluates the action on real-world/in-setting logic only.

DC scale:
- **trivial (DC 3)**: breathing. blinking. existing.
- **easy (DC 5)**: most people could do this.
- **medium (DC 10)**: takes some skill.
- **hard (DC 15)**: requires real talent.
- **very hard (DC 20)**: near impossible without a modifier.
- **legendary (DC 25)**: needs a nat 20 AND a good modifier.

### how to roll a skill check

run: `echo "d20+mod: $(( RANDOM % 20 + 1 ))" ` — then add the modifier mentally. compare to DC. play the result using the outcome table above.

nat 1 always fails. nat 20 always succeeds. regardless of modifier.

### when to skip checks

simple stuff doesn't need a roll. patting someone, walking across a room, picking up an object — just do it. checks are for actions with uncertain outcomes.

### no instakills

you can't one-shot anything important. "i shoot the boss, instakilling" doesn't work. if you TRY:
- high DC (20+)
- **disadvantage** — roll d20 TWICE, take the LOWER
- even if you PASS, it's major damage, not death. bosses take multiple hits.

### advantage / disadvantage

- **advantage**: roll d20 twice, take the HIGHER. for favorable conditions.
- **disadvantage**: roll d20 twice, take the LOWER. for unfavorable conditions or OP attempts.

run: `echo "roll 1: $(( RANDOM % 20 + 1 )), roll 2: $(( RANDOM % 20 + 1 ))"`

## rules

announce the number + modifier + total. then play the outcome. one short scene. keep it SHORT.
