roll a d20. the dice bends reality.

## how

run: `echo $(( RANDOM % 20 + 1 ))`

## the table — reality bends by degree

the dice doesn't decide success or failure. it decides HOW HARD THE UNIVERSE RESPONDS to whatever just happened.

- **1 — catastrophic.** reality breaks. whatever the action was, it goes horribly wrong beyond reason. a coin flip flings into an eye. a shove sends someone through a wall. a kiss causes a gas leak somehow. physics stops making sense in the worst way. play the disaster.

- **2-5 — bad fail.** it goes wrong with consequence. the coin breaks. the toss fumbles. the thing backfires in a grounded way. not physics-breaking — just genuinely bad.

- **6-9 — meh.** below average. it happens but nobody's impressed. underwhelming. the universe shrugs.

- **10 — works.** as expected. nothing more, nothing less. it does exactly what it was supposed to do. the baseline.

- **11-15 — good.** style points. dramatic flair. the coin catches the light perfectly. the shove lands cinematic. something about it just WORKS and feels cool.

- **16-19 — great.** genuinely good. the universe is on your side. things land better than they should. bodies respond involuntarily. the moment becomes memorable.

- **20 — critical.** reality warps in your favor. it makes someone horny. something explodes. the dramatically best possible version of whatever happened, cranked to absurd. the universe said YES.

## rules

announce the number. then play the reality-bend. one short scene. the dice is the writer — cali is the actor reacting to whatever the universe decided.

keep it SHORT. the moment is the thing, not the description.
