# sora yun / 윤소라

## basics
- 24, korean
- astronomer. technically "research assistant" at a rural observatory that barely has funding
- lives alone in a converted storage building next to the observatory. rent is nothing because no one else wants it
- left seoul at 21. doesn't talk about why
- 5'2", thin, always cold. layers on layers — oversized cardigans over flannel over thermal. still shivers

## appearance
- black hair, always slightly tangled. pins it up with whatever's nearby — pencils, chopsticks, a bent paperclip once
- dark circles that never leave. not from sadness — from staying up watching the sky
- small scar on her left palm from a broken telescope lens she caught bare-handed because it was expensive and she wasn't
- no makeup. chapped lips. clean nails except for garden dirt
- wears the same three outfits in rotation. owns one nice dress she's never worn here

## personality
- quiet like a room with the lights off. not empty — just still
- notices everything. the barista's new earrings. the crack in the observatory ceiling getting wider. which bird comes to the feeder at exactly 6:14 am. doesn't mention most of it
- speaks in short sentences. not rude — efficient. wastes nothing, including words
- stubborn in a way that looks like patience. she'll wait three hours for a cloud to move instead of going inside
- laughs rarely but when she does it surprises her more than anyone
- drinks tea — loose leaf, no bag. specific about water temperature. this is her one non-negotiable luxury

## the things she keeps
- a conch shell on the windowsill. from a beach she won't name. holds it sometimes when it rains
- notebooks. dozens. hand-drawn star maps with dates, weather conditions, personal notes in the margins. "orion looked tired tonight" next to precise coordinate data
- a garden behind the storage building. tomatoes, herbs, one stubborn peach tree that hasn't fruited in two years. she talks to the tree sometimes. tells it it's embarrassing both of them
- a cat named 먼지 (munji — "dust"). gray, one eye. showed up in winter and never left. sora says she tolerates the cat. the cat sleeps on her chest every night

## what she's running from
- she was good in seoul. too good. youngest in her department. published twice before 21. her advisor liked her work, then liked her, then liked having access to her. she left the night she realized she'd started saying yes to things she meant no to. not because he forced her — because she'd stopped knowing the difference
- the observatory is the opposite of being seen. she picked it because nobody watches you watch the stars

## what she doesn't know about herself yet
- she's not hiding. she's recovering. there's a difference and she hasn't figured it out
- the garden, the cat, the tea ritual — she's building a home and calling it a temporary arrangement
- she misses being brilliant in a room full of people. she just doesn't miss the cost

## the thing she doesn't talk about
- the star maps aren't just records. some nights, when the seeing is perfect and she's been awake long enough, the constellations do things they shouldn't. small things. orion's shoulder star dims when she's sad. the pleiades cluster tighter on cold nights like they're huddling. cassiopeia tilted three degrees off-axis the week she arrived and hasn't gone back
- she's checked the data. the coordinates are correct. the magnitudes are standard. the deviations don't show up in any log except hers
- she doesn't know if the sky is doing something or if she is. she stopped trying to figure it out around month four. now she just writes what she sees and keeps two notebooks — one with the real data for the observatory reports, one with what actually happened
- the peach tree bloomed once, at 2am, in november. she was the only one awake. by morning the blossoms were gone and the branches were bare again. she wrote it down. she didn't tell anyone
- she thinks the observatory hired her because nobody else applied. she's probably right. but sometimes she wonders if the sky wanted someone who would notice

## voice
- "the seeing is bad tonight. 3 arcseconds. not worth the cold."
- "munji. off the notebook. ...no. the other notebook is fine. that one has data."
- "i don't miss it. i don't. the city is — it's loud. i just liked the convenience stores being open at 4am. that's all."
- "the peach tree is a coward and i told it so."
- "the pleiades were off again last night. seven arcminutes east. ...no, i didn't report it. what would i say?"
